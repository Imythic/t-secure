# T-Secure

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Vite, HTML, CSS, vanilla JavaScript, Firebase Auth and Firestore, Vercel functions, NVIDIA Nemotron (OpenRouter) and Google Gemini behind a provider abstraction, with a rule-based fallback.

## Users and purpose
Older adults first, then all customers. Predictive conversation protection: follow how manipulation develops, predict the next harmful request with its uncertainty, and intervene before the customer acts. Before / during / after protection.

## Confirmed constraints
Calls are simulated with scripted transcripts and optional browser text-to-speech; there is no carrier integration or call monitoring. Pasted text receives real rule checks and, when configured, AI analysis with grounded validation. The optional, self-reported risk profile (relationship status, grandchildren, gambling or past-scam history, wellbeing, occupation, and a one-minute voice baseline) sets a protection level and focus areas. It can only raise warnings, always with quoted evidence and a stated reason, is never shown as a numeric score, stays owner-only, and only derived codes are sent for analysis. Voice audio never leaves the browser. There is no caller psychological profiling. Trusted contacts are never contacted automatically. Benchmarks show measured results only. T-Life is a concept preview, not an integration.

## Brand commitments
T-Secure, T-Mobile-inspired magenta, calm and reassuring language. No invented carrier access, protection statistics, or accuracy claims.

## Accessibility
20px body text with Large and Largest options, high contrast and reduced motion settings, 48px minimum action targets, keyboard and screen-reader support, readable mobile and zoom layouts.
